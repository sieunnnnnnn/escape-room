        AETHER-7 is a research station in low Earth orbit, sent to study material recovered
        during a deep-space survey. On 26 DEC 2059, NASA received an automated transmission:
        "CRITICAL SYSTEM FAILURE. AETHER-7 CREW EVACUATION INITIALISED." Then, five days of
        silence. On 31 DEC 2059, the station began orbiting again — but with no signs of life.
        


        Your rescue team, AETHER-8, has successfully docked. The moment you crossed the airlock,
        the docking doors sealed behind you and an emergency lockdown began: power is failing and
        oxygen is dropping fast. You have three compartments to search — the
        Docking Bay, the Trophy Room, and the Spaceship Cockpit — to find out
        what happened here, and to unlock the airlock before your air runs out.
